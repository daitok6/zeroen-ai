# ZeroEn AI

This repository is the operating knowledge base for ZeroEn.

It is used by the founder and ZeroEn's AI organization to coordinate:

- business research
- strategy
- customer discovery
- marketing
- sales
- finance
- experiments
- decisions

## Important

This repository is the company operating workspace.

It is NOT the production repository for future ZeroEn software products.

Future products should use separate repositories.

## Core Documents

- `COMPANY.md` — who ZeroEn is
- `STRATEGY.md` — what ZeroEn is currently trying to accomplish
- `PRINCIPLES.md` — how ZeroEn makes decisions

## Departments

- `research/` — market and competitor research
- `marketing/` — positioning, acquisition and content
- `sales/` — customer development and sales
- `finance/` — pricing and business economics
- `customers/` — customer interviews and insights
- `experiments/` — validation experiments
- `decisions/` — important company decisions
