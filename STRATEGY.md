# ZeroEn Strategy

Last Updated: 2026-08-24

## Current Strategic Question

What product or productized service should ZeroEn build around digital
marketing and Meta advertising expertise?

## Desired Business Characteristics

We prefer opportunities with:

1. Recurring revenue
2. High automation potential
3. Low marginal delivery cost
4. Clear customer pain
5. Demonstrable willingness to pay
6. Reasonable customer acquisition cost
7. Potential international market
8. Compatibility with English and Japanese markets
9. Ability to start small
10. Ability to evolve from service into software

## Current Leading Hypotheses

### Hypothesis A: Meta Ads Performance Audit

Provide advertisers with an actionable analysis of their Meta advertising
performance.

Potential path:

One-time audit
→ recurring monitoring
→ automated intelligence platform

### Hypothesis B: Meta Ads Intelligence / Monitoring

Continuously analyze advertising performance and explain:

- what changed
- why it may have changed
- what needs attention
- what should be tested next

Potential subscription product.

### Hypothesis C: Advertising Creative Engine

Help advertisers continuously produce and test new advertising concepts,
hooks, scripts, images, videos, and creative variations.

Potential subscription or productized service.

### Hypothesis D: Combined Ads Intelligence + Creative Engine

Use advertising performance data to determine what creative should be
produced next.

Long-term loop:

Performance Data
→ Analysis
→ Recommendation
→ Creative Generation
→ Testing
→ New Performance Data

This is currently an attractive long-term hypothesis, but it is NOT yet
validated.

## Current Target Customer Hypotheses

Potential customers may include:

- small businesses running their own Meta Ads
- small marketing teams
- ecommerce businesses
- agencies
- freelancers managing client advertising
- businesses spending meaningful amounts on Meta Ads without dedicated
  performance marketing teams

These customer segments require validation.

## Current Competitive Advantage Hypotheses

Possible advantages include:

- combination of marketing knowledge and software development
- ability to automate internal workflows
- English/Japanese market capability
- ability to begin as a service before developing software
- direct understanding of common advertiser problems

These are hypotheses, not established competitive advantages.

## Current Phase

Market Validation

## Current Objective

Identify the strongest initial customer problem before developing a
substantial product.

## Immediate Milestones

1. Establish ZeroEn AI organization
2. Research at least 10 potential business opportunities
3. Identify top 3 opportunities
4. Interview potential customers
5. Select one problem for paid validation
6. Create a simple paid offer
7. Acquire first 3 paying customers
8. Deliver manually
9. Identify repetitive work
10. Begin automation

## Current Success Metric

The immediate goal is NOT:

- website traffic
- followers
- social engagement
- number of features built

The immediate goal is:

Evidence that a specific customer will pay ZeroEn to solve a specific problem.
