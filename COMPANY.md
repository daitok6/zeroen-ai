# ZeroEn Company

## Mission

Build useful marketing products and systems that help businesses improve
their advertising performance while requiring as little manual work as
practical.

## Current Stage

ZeroEn is currently in the business validation stage.

We are not committed to a specific final product yet.

Our current areas of interest include:

- Meta Ads performance auditing
- Meta Ads monitoring and intelligence
- Advertising creative generation
- Creative performance analysis
- Marketing automation
- Related products or productized services

## Business Model Direction

The preferred path is:

Productized Service
→ Recurring Subscription
→ Increasing Automation
→ Software / SaaS

Services may be used initially to understand customers and generate revenue,
but the long-term objective is not to operate a traditional labor-intensive
agency.

## Constraints

ZeroEn is currently a side business.

Founder time is limited.

The business should therefore prioritize:

- recurring revenue
- automation
- low marginal labor
- standardized delivery
- asynchronous operations
- scalable customer acquisition

## Long-Term Objective

Build a profitable recurring-revenue business that can eventually exceed
¥1,000,000 in monthly revenue without requiring founder working hours to
increase proportionally with customer count.
