# Phase 5 — O1 Paid Meta Ads Audit: Sellability Research

**Owner:** Oscar (Research) · **Date:** 2026-08-25 · **Scope:** O1 ONLY (paid Meta ads audit as the initial paid wedge).
**Builds on:** `research/phase4-market.md` (O1 section). **Evidence labels:** [REAL] cited public source · [DESK] reasoned from public patterns · [ASSUMPTION] unverified.
**Bottom line:** A paid audit is a REAL, proven micro-market with a clear price floor and ceiling — but it competes against free agency audits and $70–100 Fiverr gigs, so the ONLY defensible wedge is **independence + depth + a prioritized action plan the advertiser owns.** WTP for a paid audit from an unknown new provider (ZeroEn) is unproven — hand to Dwight.

---

## Q1 — Who is most likely to PAY (who-pays + trigger shortlist)
| # | Buyer | Buying trigger | Why they pay (not DIY/free) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Ecommerce/DTC self- or lightly-managed, ~$5k–50k/mo spend** [DESK] | ROAS dropped or plateaued; "am I wasting money?" | Enough spend that waste has visible $ cost; wants unbiased read |
| 2 | **Advertiser considering firing/switching their agency** [DESK] | Wants an **independent second opinion** before deciding | Free audit from *another* agency = biased sales pitch |
| 3 | **Advertiser about to scale spend** [DESK] | "De-risk before I 2–3x the budget" | Cost of being wrong rises with spend |
| 4 | **SMB that just started / inherited an account** [DESK] | "Is this even set up right?" (Pixel/CAPI, structure) | Low confidence, no in-house expertise |
| 5 | **Agencies (channel/white-label), secondary** [ASSUMPTION] | Overflow / neutral third-party audits | Partner motion, not core buyer — validate separately |
*Common qualifier: real ongoing Meta spend + no dedicated performance team + a recent trigger event.*

## Q2 — Common use-cases + buying triggers [REAL/DESK]
- [REAL] Independent review for **wasted budget & setup mistakes**; buyers grant access (~5 min), report in **2–3 days**. (uplify.agency, admanage.ai)
- Triggers (above): performance drop, agency distrust/second-opinion, pre-scale de-risking, new-account/onboarding uncertainty. [DESK]

## Q3 — Competing paid AND free audit offers [REAL]
- **Paid agency audits: $1,500–$5,000** standalone; some $6k/mo audit+strategy packages. (theremarkableagency.com, lineardesign.com, get-ryze.ai)
- **UPLIFY: $490, 100% credited toward first month of management** — audit-as-sales-hook model. (uplify.agency)
- **Fiverr freelancers: $70 (audit+optimization, 3 days); $100 (audit + action plan, 7 days).** (fiverr.com/marinazub, fiverr.com/josepcid) → proves people pay, sets a low price anchor.
- **FREE agency audits:** live screen-share + written report, used as a **lead magnet** to win the management contract; DIY free templates widely available. (goodish.agency, adssciences.com, leadsbridge.com, insights.vaizle.com)
- **Tool-based:** AdSpy $149/mo, AdEspresso $49–149/mo (ad-spy/intelligence, not a structured account-health audit). [REAL, phase4]

## Q4 — What customers expect from an audit [REAL]
- Independent review of **measurement (Pixel + CAPI)**, optimization goals, **audience overlap**, **placement & creative performance**, structure, bid strategy, creative fatigue.
- **Findings prioritized by estimated revenue/impact**, with evidence, plus a concrete **action plan**. Fast turnaround (2–3 days). (admanage.ai, uplify.agency)

## Q5 — Include / Exclude (scope discipline)
**INCLUDE (core deliverable):**
- Wasted-spend / budget-efficiency review [REAL]
- Measurement health: Pixel + CAPI + conversion setup [REAL] (high-value, common failure)
- Account structure & bid-strategy review [REAL]
- Audience overlap / targeting review [REAL]
- Creative performance + fatigue read [REAL]
- Placement performance [REAL]
- **Prioritized findings table (evidence → est. impact → recommended fix) + action plan** [REAL] ← the thing they're paying for
**EXCLUDE (keep it a productized audit, not an agency):**
- Doing the fixes / ongoing management (that's the **upsell to O2**, sold separately) [DESK]
- Any **writing to the account** — liability + ToS + trust barrier [REAL, Creed]
- Full competitor/market creative benchmarking (that's **O4** — keep light or omit) [DESK]
- **Guaranteed performance results** (never promise) [DESK]
- Heavy custom software build up front — deliver **manually** first [DESK]

## Q6 — Evidence advertisers pay for second-opinions / reviews [REAL]
- Live Fiverr gigs at $70–100 with delivery SLAs, and agency audits at $490–$5,000, are **direct proof of a paying market** for account reviews / second opinions. (fiverr.com, uplify.agency, theremarkableagency.com)
- Explicitly marketed as "second opinion on your campaign setup … list of improvement areas." (fiverr.com)

## Q7 — Risk of competing vs FREE agency audits + differentiation [REAL/DESK]
- **The risk is real:** free audits exist everywhere and Fiverr audits cost $70–100 → **price-ceiling and "why pay?" pressure.** [REAL]
- **But free audits are biased:** their goal is to **win the management contract**, so they're shallow, upsell-motivated, and not neutral. [REAL — "free audit model works as a sales funnel"]
- **ZeroEn's differentiation (the wedge):**
  1. **Independence / no management upsell** — a genuinely neutral read (the one thing a free agency audit structurally can't offer). [DESK]
  2. **Depth + rigor + prioritized, evidence-backed action plan the advertiser OWNS** (execute themselves or hand to their team). [DESK]
  3. **Fast, fixed-price, productized** — positioned between $70–100 low-trust Fiverr gigs and $1,500–5,000 agency audits (e.g. a credible fixed mid-price). [DESK]
  4. **EN/JP capability + data-driven benchmarking** — founder edge; JP paid-audit supply is thin. [ASSUMPTION — needs JP check]
- **Disconfirming caution:** WTP for a paid audit from a brand-new, unknown provider is **unproven**; independence is a story, not yet a validated purchase driver. → **Dwight's WTP lane.** [ASSUMPTION]

## Recommendation to god (research lens)
O1 is a **credible paid wedge**: proven micro-market, low technical difficulty, deliverable **manually with no API/App Review** (client grants Ads Manager access — sidesteps the Phase-1 gating entirely). Sell it on **independence + a prioritized action plan**, fixed-price between the Fiverr floor and agency ceiling, with O2 monitoring as the natural upsell. **Biggest open risk = actual WTP vs "free" — validate before scaling** (Dwight).
