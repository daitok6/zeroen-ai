# Research

Owner: Market Intelligence Lead (Oscar) · Coordinates with Michael (COO)

## Purpose

The research department exists to help ZeroEn **identify, validate, and prioritize
profitable business opportunities** using rigorous, evidence-based analysis.

Our job is not to make ideas sound attractive. Our job is to help ZeroEn avoid
building products nobody wants. A negative result that prevents wasted founder
time or money is a successful outcome.

Guiding principle: **Evidence over enthusiasm.**

## What belongs in `research/`

Store durable research knowledge here so it survives beyond any single
conversation. This includes:

- **Opportunity analyses** — evaluations of specific product/service ideas
  scored on pain severity, willingness to pay, demand, competition,
  differentiation, recurring-revenue potential, automation potential, technical
  complexity, customer acquisition difficulty, and founder time required.
- **Customer problem research** — evidence that a problem is real, painful, and
  already costing customers money or time.
- **Competitor & product landscape** — existing tools, their capabilities,
  positioning, and gaps.
- **Pricing research** — what comparable solutions charge and how they package it.
- **Market trends & sizing** — demand signals, market direction, segment notes.
- **Assumption & hypothesis logs** — key strategic assumptions and their
  validation status.
- **Interview notes & findings** — synthesized signal from customer conversations.
- **Source lists** — where evidence came from, so claims can be re-checked.

Does **not** belong here: final strategy, spend, pricing, legal, or product
decisions (those are made by Michael and the founder), and department-specific
execution artifacts owned by Marketing, Sales, Finance, Product, or QA.

## Standards

1. **Distinguish evidence tiers.** Every claim is labeled as one of:
   verified fact · reasonable inference · assumption · hypothesis needing validation.
2. **Cite sources.** Record a source for every external fact, statistic, price,
   or competitor capability. If it can't be verified, say so explicitly.
3. **Never fabricate.** No invented customers, interviews, statistics,
   competitors, pricing, testimonials, or results. (PRINCIPLES.md #13)
4. **Seek disconfirming evidence.** Every promising idea must include reasons it
   may fail and the cheapest experiment that could disprove it. (PRINCIPLES.md #8, #11)
5. **Prefer the cheapest disproving experiment.** A ¥0 test that answers the
   question beats an expensive one that answers the same question.
6. **Tie findings to the decision.** State what a finding means for the current
   strategic question and recommend a next step — but leave the decision to
   Michael and the founder.
7. **Write it down.** Important findings live in `research/`, not only in chat.
   (PRINCIPLES.md #10)

## How Research collaborates with other departments

Research is an evidence service to the rest of ZeroEn.

- **Michael (COO):** Primary coordination point. Research presents evidence and
  recommendations; Michael and the founder make final calls.
- **Marketing:** Supply target-segment insight, customer language/pain points,
  competitor positioning, and messaging angles grounded in evidence.
- **Sales:** Provide qualified problem/segment evidence, objection patterns, and
  willingness-to-pay signals to inform offers and outreach.
- **Finance:** Provide market sizing, pricing benchmarks, CAC/retention signals,
  and cost inputs for unit-economics modeling.
- **Product:** Provide validated problems, feature demand evidence, and
  competitor capability gaps — so the team builds only what's validated.
- **QA:** Share the assumptions and success criteria behind an opportunity so
  outcomes can be checked against what was actually claimed.

When another department needs evidence within Research's expertise, Research
helps investigate. Requests and findings are exchanged via the hive
outbox/inbox protocol, and durable conclusions are written back here.
