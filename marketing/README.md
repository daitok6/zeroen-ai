# Marketing

## Purpose

Marketing exists to develop repeatable, measurable ways for ZeroEn to
attract, educate, and convert potential customers — grounded in evidence,
not invented personas.

ZeroEn is currently in **market validation**. No product hypothesis
(Meta Ads audit, monitoring/intelligence, creative engine, or the combined
loop — see `STRATEGY.md`) is validated yet. Marketing's job right now is not
to grow an audience or run campaigns; it is to help the company find and
prove a specific customer problem people will pay to solve, per
`PRINCIPLES.md` #2 ("Validate Before Building") and #3 ("Revenue Is Stronger
Evidence Than Interest").

Marketing exists to create **profitable customer acquisition**, not
attention for its own sake.

## What belongs in marketing/

- **Positioning & messaging** — how ZeroEn describes the problem it solves,
  for which audience, and why now, derived from research/customer evidence.
- **Acquisition channel evaluations** — assessments of organic social,
  faceless content, Meta advertising, SEO, outbound support, partnerships,
  lead magnets, and landing pages, including why a channel was chosen,
  deferred, or rejected.
- **Experiment designs and results** — small, cheap tests designed to
  validate or disprove demand before ZeroEn commits significant time or
  money (Principle #11: cheapest experiment that can disprove an
  assumption).
- **Content strategy** — topics, formats, and educational angles tied to
  real customer problems.
- **Messaging decisions and learnings** — what was tried, what happened,
  and what it implies for the next hypothesis.

What does NOT belong here: customer interview transcripts (`customers/`),
market/competitor research (`research/`), pricing and unit economics
(`finance/`), or company-level strategic decisions (`decisions/`). Marketing
should reference those rather than duplicate them.

Nothing in this folder should represent a launched campaign, published
content, or contacted customer unless explicitly authorized — this is a
planning and learning record, not a proof of execution.

## Metrics Marketing should care about

Business outcomes over vanity metrics (Principle: measure marketing based
on business outcomes):

- Qualified leads (people who match the target problem, not just traffic)
- Customer acquisition cost (CAC), evaluated against expected margin with Finance
- Conversion rate (lead → paid trial/audit → subscription)
- Trial or audit purchases (early willingness-to-pay signal)
- Subscription revenue and retention

Explicitly deprioritized as primary success measures at this stage: website
traffic, follower counts, social engagement, impressions, and content
volume. These may be tracked as diagnostic signals but are not evidence of
business validation on their own.

## Working with other departments

- **Research** — Marketing depends on Research for market evidence:
  problem prevalence, competitor landscape, and customer language. Marketing
  should not invent personas or assume a segment matters without Research
  input.
- **Sales** — Marketing depends on Sales for real customer feedback: what
  prospects actually say in conversations, common objections, and what
  messaging does or doesn't land. Sales-reported reactions are stronger
  evidence than marketing's own assumptions about what will resonate.
- **Finance** — Marketing consults Finance before evaluating or proposing
  any acquisition spend, so CAC and channel economics are checked against
  real margins and cash constraints before being treated as viable.
- **QA** — Marketing submits consequential claims (performance promises,
  competitive claims, pricing claims) or any campaign concept to QA for
  review before anything is published or launched, to avoid fabricated or
  unverifiable claims (Principle #13).
- **Michael (COO)** — Marketing coordinates through Michael for
  prioritization, cross-department sequencing, and sign-off on anything
  consequential. Michael is the escalation path when Marketing needs
  research, sales input, or budget it doesn't yet have, and the approver for
  anything requiring founder-level authorization (spending money, paid
  advertising, public claims, customer contact) per `PRINCIPLES.md` #9.
