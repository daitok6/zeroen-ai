## 11. Paid-vs-Free Explanation

> **"Why should I pay ¥50,000 when agencies audit my account for free?"**
>
> Because free isn't actually free — it's paid for differently. When an agency runs a free audit, someone still pays for that analyst's time; if it isn't you, it's the retainer they're hoping to win afterward. That's not a knock on every agency — plenty of the people doing free audits are competent and mean well — but the incentive built into a free audit is still to find *enough* to justify signing you, not necessarily to hand you the complete, unflattering picture, especially if part of the problem is the agency's own earlier work.
>
> Paying for this audit changes what I have to gain from it. There's no retainer waiting on the other side of the report, so there's nothing pulling the findings toward "sign here" instead of toward what's actually true. You're not paying for something better than what a free audit *could* be in theory — you're paying so the incentive points at your interest instead of the analyst's.

## 12. Sample Audit Finding

**[SAMPLE / DEMONSTRATION — illustrative example of report format and depth. Not a real customer's data or a real audit result.]**

**Finding: Conversions API not implemented — pixel-only measurement is likely undercounting conversions**
- **What we found:** The account relies on browser-side Pixel tracking only; no server-side Conversions API (CAPI). Recent iOS/browser tracking restrictions mean pixel-only setups typically miss a meaningful share of real conversions — the platform is optimizing against incomplete data.
- **Estimated impact:** High. Incomplete measurement distorts every downstream decision (what looks like it's working, where budget shifts, which creative gets scaled). Usually one of the highest-leverage fixes because it affects every other number.
- **Recommended action:** Set up CAPI alongside the existing Pixel, matched on the same event names and dedup keys so signals reconcile rather than double-count. One-time technical setup, often achievable without a developer via a supported e-commerce platform's native integration.
- **Why prioritized first:** Fixing structure or creative before measurement means optimizing against a partially blind signal — order matters.
