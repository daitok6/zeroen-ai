## Honesty note on Part B (read before using)

Per PRINCIPLES.md #13, I have **not** fabricated any named individual or company. I also ran targeted
public searches this pass for a real, citable "person/company X publicly shows the ICP + a trigger"
candidate and **found none clean enough to cite** — the searches surfaced generic industry blog
content, not an identifiable, specific prospect.

Beyond that search result, I'm making a **deliberate judgment call**: even where a specific real
individual's public post *could* be found (e.g., a pseudonymous Reddit/forum user complaining about
their Meta Ads), I think naming that person as a sales "target" — with fields like "why they fit" and
"trigger observed" — crosses from ordinary ICP research into building an identifying profile of a
private person for commercial contact, which is a meaningfully different act than what most people
posting under a handle would expect. That risk (to the person, and to ZeroEn's reputation if this
document were ever seen) isn't worth it for a 3-slot pilot. **Company-level** public leads (a named
business's own public statement, e.g. in a case study or the business's own account) would be a
different, lower-risk case — none surfaced this pass, but if one had, I would have cited it directly
as a Part B entry.

So Part B below is **search recipes only** — a venue plus a specific signal to look for — not named
individuals. The founder (who can exercise real judgment about a specific real person before
deciding to reach out) is the right one to do that last step, not this agent.
